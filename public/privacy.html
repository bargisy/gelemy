Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Gelemy. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how it is used, and how it is stored when you use our application.

2. Local-First Data Architecture

Gelemy is designed as a "local-first" application. We do not own, operate, or maintain any centralized database servers. We do not transmit your financial data, projects, or personal information to our own servers. All your data is stored locally on your device and synchronized directly with your personal Google Drive account.

3. Google API Services Usage

Gelemy uses Google OAuth 2.0 to authenticate you and connect to your Google Drive. We request the minimum necessary permissions (Scopes) to operate:

  • Google Drive Files: We only access, create, and edit Google Sheets spreadsheets that were explicitly created by Gelemy. We cannot read or modify any other files in your Google Drive.
  • App Data: We store a tiny configuration file in a hidden App Data folder to link your device to your spreadsheet.

Gelemy's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

Because we do not collect or store your data on our servers, we have no data to share, sell, or rent to third parties, advertisers, or analytics companies.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You have full control over your data. To delete your data permanently:

  • Open your Google Drive and delete the "Gelemy Tracker" spreadsheet.
  • Click "Disconnect Google Account" within the Gelemy app settings to clear your local device cache.
  • Remove Gelemy's access from your Google Account Security settings.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
